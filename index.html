Questions

Before you start

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me? +

Yes. Your plan starts from where you are today, not where an athlete is. You don't need to be fit to start.

I have back, knee or shoulder pain. +

Tell me in the form. We assess it first and train around it, then build it up. If something needs a doctor or a scan first, I'll tell you before we start.

I don't have a gym. +

Online plans can be built for home with little equipment. In Alexandria, I can also train you at your home.

How much does it cost? +

Online coaching from $60 (3,400 EGP) a month. Training at Kraft from 1,800 EGP a month. You'll know the exact price before you pay anything.

How fast will I see results? +

It depends on where you start and how consistent you are, so I won't promise a number. What I do promise is that your progress is measured and checked, so we always know what's working.

Do you coach in Arabic? +

Yes. Arabic or English, whichever you prefer.
